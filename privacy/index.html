Roundly Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-03

Roundly ("the app") is a circular daily planner made by SDDT (Seonghun Lee). This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: we do not collect any data.

Data stored on your device

  • Your routines, blocks, templates and settings are stored only on your device (and, on iPhone, in a shared app container so that widgets and your paired Apple Watch can show your ring). They are never uploaded to us.
  • If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

Calendar (optional)

If you turn on "Show calendar events", the app asks for permission to read your calendars. Today's events are read on your device to draw them on the ring. Event data is never stored by the app, copied, or sent anywhere. You can revoke access at any time in system settings.

Notifications (optional)

Block-start reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No notification content leaves your device.

Purchases

Roundly Premium is sold through the App Store or Google Play and processed by Apple or Google. We use RevenueCat to check whether your purchase is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipt from the store; it does not receive your name, email or schedule. See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
